# Project overview

The objective is to build or acquire a technology-native, third-party multifamily property-management platform in the United States through an acquisition-led roll-up plus organic growth, targeting institutional scale.

The economic thesis is to acquire property-management companies, rationalize redundant seller payroll where evidence supports it, use technology, automation, and nearshoring, and selectively add adjacent services while preserving controls and owner trust.

Underwriting must distinguish acquired economics from buyer-created economics. The platform remains focused on third-party management and must not assume away the risk that owners internalize management. The goal is a credible institutional underwriting case, not a promotional case.
