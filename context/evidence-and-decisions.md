# Evidence and decisions

## Taxonomy

- `[F]` Fact
- `[C]` Comparable
- `[E]` External evidence
- `[A]` Assumption
- `[OQ]` Open question
- Jack principal decision

Every material underwriting driver must carry one of these classifications. Assumptions cannot be promoted to facts. Arithmetic plugs are assumptions unless independently evidenced. Target-level payroll or revenue evidence overrides generic assumptions. Evidence conflicts require escalation. No material economic inference may be introduced silently.
